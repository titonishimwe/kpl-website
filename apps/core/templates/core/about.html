Who We Are

Kigali Public Library (KPL) is a public-serving institution that oversees and supports more than 100 libraries across Rwanda, expanding access to books, digital resources, and learning opportunities beyond the city of Kigali.

As a custodian of knowledge and culture, KPL convenes readers, students, professionals, creatives, and families within an inclusive environment designed for discovery, dialogue, and personal growth.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To create a world where all communities have access to information and learning opportunities that meet their creativity, curiosity, and intellectual aspirations.

Mission

To provide a dynamic and inclusive national platform that fosters lifelong learning, cultural enrichment, and community transformation.
