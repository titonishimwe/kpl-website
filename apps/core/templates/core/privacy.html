Privacy Policy

Kigali Public Library is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect and use information.

Last updated: February 2026

Information We Collect

While using our service or registering for membership, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including:

  • Email address and phone number
  • First and last name
  • Date of birth and gender
  • National identification or passport number
  • Residential address (District, Sector, Village)
  • Parent/guardian details for junior applicants under 18

Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for website operation and analytics cookies (in anonymized form) to understand website usage and improve public service delivery. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies at any time.

Use of Data

Your data is strictly utilized to provide and maintain library accounts, issue library cards, facilitate book borrowing and room bookings, process communications, and ensure compliance with public institution guidelines.

Data Security & Contact

We implement security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. For any inquiries regarding data protection, please contact:
